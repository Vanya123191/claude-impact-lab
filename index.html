Gap Finder

For a nonprofit finance lead or a donor. The family’s price is capped by income, so the gap is what is left. This tool derives that price from the state formula, shows which income table it used, and shows what a rate change or a gift does to one home’s bill.

Lane 3 (Fund it). Default deal is the synthetic one-home example from the lab repo, not a real project. Every number is labeled sourced or assumed.

Sources and assumptions


Sourced: price cap formula from California Health and Safety Code 50052.5 (very low: 30% x 50% of area median income; lower: 30% x 70%; moderate: 35% x 110%, and not under 28% of gross income). Household size factors 0.7 / 0.8 / 0.9 / 1.0 / 1.08 for 1 to 5 persons, with a 3-bedroom treated as 4 persons. Mortgage rate 7.18% is Freddie Mac 30-year, 2026-11-05, and 7.40% on 2026-10-08 (FRED MORTGAGE30US, as listed in the lab data sources). Income table dates are from HCD’s 2026 State Income Limits memo (revised June 23, 2026) and the separate State HOME table (effective June 1, 2026). Program statuses are from the lab data-sources file, checked November 6, 2026.
Derived, confirm: the 4-person area median income of $138,600 is the Irvine Company’s published 2026 Orange County 120% limit of $166,300 divided by 1.2. It was not read from the HCD PDF. 
Assumed: down payment 5%, property tax 1.1%, insurance $2,400 a year, HOA $200 a month, 30-year term, no mortgage insurance, and every cost and subsidy line (synthetic example). Public subsidies and rent earned are placeholders from the example, not real programs.
